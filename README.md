## 🚀 Featured Project

<div align="center">

<a href="https://github.com/nps-genomics/GenoBridge">
  <img src="https://img.shields.io/static/v1?label=Featured%20Project&message=GenoBridge&color=0f172a&labelColor=3fb950&style=for-the-badge" alt="GenoBridge badge" />
</a>

</div>

**GenoBridge** is a sample-size-adaptive ensemble ML pipeline for genotype-to-phenotype prediction from SNP data in VCF format. It addresses three limitations in current genomic prediction workflows:

- 🔷 **Adaptive model selection** — chooses the model class (Ridge, Random Forest, XGBoost, MLP, stacking ensemble) according to cohort size, across four sample-size regimes
- 🔷 **CV-R² diagnostics** — separates true predictive generalization from rank-order preservation
- 🔷 **ML-gated GWAS** — concentrates statistical power on traits that show evidence of genomic predictability

> 🧬 Benchmarked against GBLUP on five plant datasets spanning **n = 136 to n = 10,729** (*Arabidopsis*, rice, grape, wheat) with nested cross-validation and bootstrapped confidence intervals · pip-installable · manuscript under review at *Plant Methods*

## 🌿 Active Research Areas

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🍊 Citrus Genomics
Haplotype-resolved, chromosome-level nuclear genome of **'Rio Red' grapefruit** from PacBio HiFi, plus complete plastomes of three red-fleshed cultivars (*Rio Red, Ruby Red, Texas Red*). Resolving IR boundary polymorphism, ancestry composition, and regulatory control of lycopene accumulation.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🌾 Wheat Pangenomics
Assembling and analyzing pangenomes of Montana winter, spring, and durum wheat from **whole-genome resequencing**. Characterizing selective sweeps, genomic diversity, and functional gene expansion across elite North American germplasm.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🔗 Cross-Kingdom sRNA Regulation
Investigating **microRNA-mediated cross-kingdom gene silencing** between wheat and wheat stem sawfly (*Cephus cinctus*) — host-derived sRNAs as a route to crop resistance.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🫘 Legume Genomics & Epigenomics
Genome sequencing, DNA methylation landscapes, and **transcriptome-wide association mapping** in chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*) and ricebean (*Vigna umbellata*).

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🍄 Fungal Genomics & Pathogenicity
Evolution of pathogenicity-associated genes in **Rhizoctonia solani AG1-IA** through genome duplication and transposon-mediated gene-function alteration; genome compartmentalization and effector variation in *Zasmidium citri-griseum*.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🧫 Organellar Genomics
**Plastid-to-mitochondria gene transfer** and repeat-mediated mitochondrial genome reconfiguration in grapefruit, developed as organellar resources for **HLB (citrus greening)** research.

</td>
